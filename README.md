## What you give up at this price

Cheap VPS hosting always has a "cheap" side of the ledger, and it's better to know it upfront than discover it at 2 a.m.

- **It's unmanaged.** RackNerd's 24/7 support covers the platform — network, hardware, the panel — but you administer the OS yourself. If "SSH into a server" sounds like a chore rather than a feature, a managed host or shared hosting is a better fit.
- **No included backups.** Nothing in the plan specs mentions automated backups, so treat your data accordingly — snapshot to off-site storage yourself.
- **Custom ISOs go through a support ticket.** Standard OS choices at order time are CentOS, Rocky Linux, AlmaLinux, Fedora, Debian, and Ubuntu. Installing from your own media is possible via the HTML5 VNC console, but it's a ticket-assisted process.
- **IPv6 is location-limited.** Up to 100 free IPv6 addresses are available, but currently only in Los Angeles and France, and only on request after ordering.
- **Network quality varies by route.** The Los Angeles plans are marketed as Asia-optimized with Noction IRP routing, but "optimized" is a routing claim, not a latency guarantee to your specific ISP. This is exactly what the $17.99 monthly test period is for.
